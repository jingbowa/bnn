# BNN estimator website

Register: brand, educational research resource.

Visitors are researchers, students, teachers, and software users seeking an understandable
nonparametric estimator, formal statistical properties, and reproducible computation.

Purpose: explain and promote informed use of BNN. The opening centers on its construction.
Source attribution and maintenance credit connect the resource to the researchers.
The launch is statistical; it does not contain price demonstrations.

The central interaction follows one query through subsampling, prediction, averaging,
and exact rank weights. Secondary controls expose scale choice and signed two-scale weights.

All pages contain real content. Static explanation is available without JavaScript.
Scientific captions and result scopes are specific. Complete authorship is given in Papers.
